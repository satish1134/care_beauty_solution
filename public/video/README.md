# CARe Beauty Solution — Hero Video Asset Guide

To replace the fallback cinematic campaign sequence with the official video files:

### File Paths
- **Desktop Video**: `/public/video/care-hero-desktop.mp4` (H.264/H.265 or WebM, 1080p, 24/30fps, 8–15s loop, muted, ~3–8MB)
- **Mobile Video**: `/public/video/care-hero-mobile.mp4` (Vertical/Square 4:5 or 9:16 optimized for mobile, ~1–3MB)
- **Poster Image**: `/public/images/hero.png` (or `.webp`)

### Video Requirements
- **Duration**: 8–15 seconds
- **Seamless Loop**: The final frame must match or smoothly dissolve into the first frame.
- **Audio**: Audio is stripped or muted. Autoplay uses `muted playsInline loop`.
- **Content Storyboard**:
  1. 0–3s: Extreme close-up of natural Indian skin texture, hydrated and radiant in warm light.
  2. 3–6s: Slow rotation of the CARe amber vessel catching champagne gold reflections.
  3. 6–9s: Elegant absorption / velvet barrier melt with no artificial shimmer.
  4. 9–12s: The Core Trio arranged together with soft gold rim lighting.
  5. 12–15s: Settling on the hero composition in deep forest green (#082417).
